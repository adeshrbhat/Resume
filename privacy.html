Privacy policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

This website is the personal portfolio of Adesh R Bhat ("I", "me"), based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. This policy explains what information is handled when you visit the site or contact me.

What the site collects

The site has no accounts, sign-up forms, comment sections or contact form. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not load fonts, scripts or other content from third-party servers. It does not store anything in your browser.

Hosting and server logs

The site is delivered by a hosting provider. Hosting providers commonly record technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and the time of each request in server logs. I do not use this data to identify or profile visitors. The hosting provider handles it under its own privacy policy.

When you contact me

If you email or call me, I receive the contact details and the message content you choose to share. I use them only to respond to you. I keep them for as long as needed for that conversation, or longer if the law requires. Email is handled by your email provider and mine.

Links to other sites

The site links to services such as GitHub and LinkedIn. I do not control them, and their own privacy practices apply once you leave this site.

Children

The site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

To the extent provided by applicable law in India, you can ask what personal information I hold about you from our correspondence, ask me to correct or delete it, and withdraw any consent you have given. Email me and I will respond.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when the current version took effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to adeshrbhat@gmail.com.
